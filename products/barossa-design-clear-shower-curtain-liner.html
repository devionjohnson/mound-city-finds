Barossa Design Clear Shower Curtain Liner, 72"

What it is

The Barossa Design liner is a clear 72-inch shower curtain liner, a standard size for many tub-and-shower combos. It is made of PEVA, a lightweight plastic often used as a vinyl alternative, with rustproof grommets at the top and magnets along the bottom hem to help the liner hang straighter and stay closer to the tub. A clear liner is usually paired with a decorative curtain on the same rod, or used on its own if you want a simple splash barrier.

Who it is for

This is a practical restock if your current liner is stained, torn, or starting to smell musty, and for renters who want a waterproof layer behind a fabric curtain they already like. If your shower needs a longer stall curtain, or you want an all-in-one printed curtain, confirm the length and style on Amazon. This page is about the clear 72-inch liner.

Features to check before you buy

  • The finished length and width against your tub or stall opening. Seventy-two inches is common, but some stalls need longer.
  • Grommet spacing so the liner lines up with the rings you already have.
  • Magnets or weighted hems, which help reduce billowing from the shower spray.
  • Whether the listing describes the liner as PEVA and any current certification or care notes, such as OEKO-TEX, which can change by batch.
  • How it should be cleaned or replaced. Most lightweight liners are easier to wipe or replace than to treat as a long-term textile.

Pros

  • A clear liner lets a decorative curtain show through, so you can change the look without replacing the waterproof layer every time.
  • Rustproof grommets are a better match for a damp bathroom than plain punched holes.
  • Bottom magnets help the liner stay closer to the tub instead of drifting inward.
  • A standard 72-inch size is easy to pair with commonly sold shower curtain rings.

Tradeoffs

  • PEVA liners are not as substantial as a heavy fabric curtain and can wrinkle or cling in a way some people dislike.
  • Clear plastic will show soap scum, so it needs more frequent wiping than a dark or patterned curtain.
  • Lightweight liners wear out. This is a replaceable bathroom supply, not a forever fixture.
  • If your rod is set for a longer stall curtain, a 72-inch liner may not reach the tub or floor as you expect.

How it compares

A clear PEVA liner is doing a different job than a decorative fabric shower curtain. Many bathrooms use both: the liner inside for water, the fabric outside for color. If you want one piece instead of two, look for a waterproof curtain that does not need a separate liner. They are not substitutes, just other everyday household items on this shelf.

Buying tip

Measure from the rod down to just inside the tub before you order, and count your rings so the grommets will line up with the hardware you already have.
