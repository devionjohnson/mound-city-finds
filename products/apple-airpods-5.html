Apple AirPods 5 with Active Noise Cancellation

See today’s price at Amazon (opens in a new tab)

What it is

Apple AirPods 5 with active noise cancellation are the standard AirPods model in this generation, with ANC, spatial audio, and a USB-C charging case. They sit in a more everyday wireless tier than AirPods Pro, while still covering the noise-cancelling and spatial-audio features many commuters want. Unlike the Pro models, standard AirPods use the familiar open stem-and-bud shape rather than sealing silicone tips. Confirm the exact generation and case details on the current Amazon page.

Who it is for

These AirPods suit people who want wireless earbuds with noise cancellation, but prefer the regular AirPods fit, or want a step below the Pro line. They are a common commute and call option for iPhone users. They are less of a match if you want the stronger in-ear seal of Pro tips, or if you only need a wired pair for a USB-C phone.

Features to check before you buy

  • That this is the AirPods 5 model with active noise cancellation, not an older generation without ANC.
  • The open AirPods fit, with no silicone tips to swap.
  • Spatial audio and the USB-C charging case.
  • Compatibility and software requirements on the current product page.
  • What is in the box, including cable type, which can vary by listing.

Pros

  • Active noise cancellation on a standard AirPods shape is useful if you dislike silicone tips.
  • USB-C charging matches newer cables and chargers.
  • Spatial audio and everyday wireless pairing are included in this tier, according to the product description.
  • The fit can feel lighter for people who never got used to in-ear tips.

Tradeoffs

  • An open fit generally isolates less than a well-sealed Pro-style tip, even with ANC turned on.
  • You still have a case to charge and keep track of.
  • They will not plug in as a wired backup the way EarPods do.
  • You should confirm the generation on the listing, because AirPods names and features have changed across years.

How it compares

Stack them this way: USB-C EarPods for a wired pair with no case, AirPods 5 for wireless ANC in the standard shape, and AirPods Pro 3 for in-ear tips and the flagship feature set. If you are happy with the classic AirPods feel and just want noise cancellation, AirPods 5 are the middle path. If you already know you want a tight seal, look at Pro 3 first.

Buying tip

Read the listing title and specs to confirm you are buying AirPods 5 with ANC and a USB-C case, not an earlier lookalike photo or a different generation.
