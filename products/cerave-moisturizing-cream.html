CeraVe Moisturizing Cream, 19 oz

What it is

CeraVe Moisturizing Cream in the 19-ounce tub is a fragrance-free cream with ceramides and hyaluronic acid, made for face and body and often used on dry skin. The large tub is a household-size restock rather than a travel jar. It is a rich cream, so people who want a lighter gel or an oil-free face lotion may prefer a different texture, including other products in the same brand family.

Who it is for

This cream suits people with dry skin who want one unscented moisturizer they can use on the body and, if it agrees with them, on the face. It is also a common household tub for winter dryness on hands, legs, and arms. It is less ideal if you want a scented body lotion, a very light daily face gel, or a small tube for a gym bag.

Features to check before you buy

  • The 19-ounce tub size, which is large and meant to stay at home.
  • Fragrance-free formula, with ceramides and hyaluronic acid as the commonly highlighted ingredients.
  • That it is labeled for face and body, though you should still follow the package and your own skin's needs.
  • The rich cream texture, which is heavier than a lotion.
  • How you dip into the tub. Some people prefer a clean spatula to keep the jar tidy.

Pros

  • A fragrance-free cream is easier to live with in a scent-sensitive household.
  • The large tub is a practical restock for body dryness.
  • Ceramides and hyaluronic acid are familiar moisturizing ingredients in this kind of cream.
  • One tub can cover more than a single small face jar would.

Tradeoffs

  • A rich cream can feel heavy or greasy on oily skin or in hot weather.
  • A tub is less convenient and less hygienic-feeling for some people than a pump bottle.
  • Nineteen ounces is bulky for travel.
  • If you only want a lightweight face moisturizer, this may be more cream than you need.

How it compares

The closest face-focused alternative on this site is the La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair face moisturizer, an oil-free, fragrance-free daily face cream that is usually lighter than a big tub of CeraVe cream. If you want a scented body lotion instead, see the eos Shea Better Vanilla Cashmere lotion. Choose CeraVe for a rich, unscented face-and-body cream; choose La Roche-Posay if you want a lighter face cream; choose eos if the fragrance is the point.

Buying tip

If your skin is oily or you only need a daytime face lotion, consider the lighter La Roche-Posay cream first. If you are buying for dry arms, legs, and winter skin, the 19-ounce CeraVe tub is the more practical size.
