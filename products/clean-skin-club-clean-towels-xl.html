What it is

Clean Skin Club Clean Towels XL are a 50-count pack of single-use, extra-soft face towels, described as USDA biobased, for drying skin or taking off leftover cleanser without a cloth towel. The XL size is meant to feel closer to a regular washcloth than a thin wipe. They are a disposable drying towel, not a makeup-remover wipe soaked in cleanser, and not a reusable terrycloth cloth.

Who it is for

These towels suit people who want a clean towel each time, such as after a workout, after makeup removal, or if they are trying to avoid a damp cloth towel sitting in a bathroom. They also appeal to people who share a bathroom and want a fresh texture on their face. They are less ideal if you want to avoid disposable products, or if you need a wipe that already contains makeup remover.

Features to check before you buy

  • The 50-count pack and the XL size, which is larger than a typical thin facial wipe.
  • That these are dry towels, not pre-soaked remover wipes.
  • The USDA biobased description on the current listing, and any disposal or composting notes the maker provides.
  • Texture notes: they are meant to be soft enough for the face.
  • Storage, so the remaining towels stay clean in the pack.

Pros

  • A fresh towel each time can feel cleaner than a repeatedly used cloth.
  • The XL size is more like a washcloth than a small wipe.
  • They can dry the face or help lift leftover cleanser without adding a lotion or scent.
  • A 50-count pack is a regular restock rather than a trial envelope.

Tradeoffs

  • Single-use towels create ongoing waste and cost compared with a washed cloth.
  • They do not, by themselves, remove stubborn waterproof makeup the way a remover wipe or oil can.
  • You need storage space for a 50-count pack.
  • If your goal is makeup removal, a dedicated remover may still be necessary.

How it compares

The closest product on this site is the Neutrogena Makeup Remover Wipes twin pack. Those are pre-soaked wipes meant to lift makeup, including many waterproof formulas, without a rinse. Clean Towels are dry and are mainly for drying or a last wipe on clean skin. Many people who wear makeup might use a remover first and a clean towel after, rather than choosing only one. Daily moisturizers such as CeraVe Moisturizing Cream come after cleansing, not instead of a towel.

Buying tip

Decide whether your problem is a dirty cloth towel or leftover makeup. If it is makeup, start with a remover; if it is the towel itself, a pack of dry XL towels is the more direct answer.
