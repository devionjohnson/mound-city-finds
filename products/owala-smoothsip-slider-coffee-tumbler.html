Owala SmoothSip Slider Coffee Tumbler, 12 oz, Vanilla Bean

See today’s price at Amazon (opens in a new tab)

What it is

The Owala SmoothSip Slider is a compact 12-ounce insulated tumbler in a vanilla bean finish, made for coffee, tea, or a short iced drink rather than a full day's water. The slider lid covers the sip opening, which is easier to manage in a cup holder than a loose, always-open sip gap. At 12 ounces, it is closer to a standard mug than to a large water bottle, and it is meant to keep a single drink warm or cold on a commute or at a desk.

Who it is for

This tumbler is for people who want a smaller insulated cup for one coffee or iced drink, especially in the car. It is not a substitute for a 24- or 32-ounce water bottle. If you mainly want all-day water, look at a larger bottle. If you want a big travel mug that holds two coffees, a 12-ounce cup may feel small.

Features to check before you buy

  • The 12-ounce capacity, which is a single regular drink, not a jumbo cup.
  • The slider lid and whether it feels easy to open with one hand.
  • Insulation notes and whether the listing is for hot drinks, cold drinks, or both.
  • Fit in a cup holder, and whether the tumbler is designed to sit stable in a console.
  • The vanilla bean color and how the lid comes apart for washing.

Pros

  • A smaller size is easier to carry when you only want one drink.
  • A slider lid is often tidier in a bag or car than an open sip tumbler.
  • Insulation helps a short coffee stay warm, or an iced drink stay cold, on a commute.
  • The light vanilla bean finish is easy to pair with other bottles if you already carry an Owala.

Tradeoffs

  • Twelve ounces will feel small if you are used to a large cafe cup or a refillable 16- to 20-ounce mug.
  • It is not a water-bottle replacement for all-day hydration.
  • Slider lids still need regular cleaning around the moving part.
  • Finish and lid details can differ by listing, so confirm you are looking at the 12-ounce SmoothSip model.

How it compares

This tumbler sits beside, not in place of, the Owala FreeSip 24-ounce water bottle. The FreeSip is for water and mixed sipping; the SmoothSip is a smaller coffee-style cup with a slider. The HydroJug Traveler is larger still, at 32 ounces, and is built more like a water bottle with a flip straw and handle. If you only want one vessel, decide whether your daily drink is mostly water or mostly coffee, then pick the size that matches.

Buying tip

If your usual coffee order is larger than 12 ounces, check the size before you buy, or plan on a smaller pour at home so the drink actually fits with a little room at the top.
