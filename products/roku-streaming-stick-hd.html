Roku Streaming Stick HD with Voice Remote

See today’s price at Amazon (opens in a new tab)

What it is

The Roku Streaming Stick HD is a compact HD streaming stick that draws power from the television and adds apps, free channels, and a voice remote to a set that may not have those services built in. It is an affordable way to update an older television without buying a new one. It is an HD model, not a 4K stick, so the picture resolution it can output is high definition, not ultra high definition.

Who it is for

This stick is for households with an HD television, or anyone who wants Roku's interface and does not need 4K. It is a practical upgrade for a guest room TV, a bedroom set, or an older living-room television. It is the wrong pick if your TV and content are 4K and you want that resolution, or if you are already committed to a Fire TV or other software ecosystem and do not want a second interface.

Features to check before you buy

  • That this is an HD stick, not a 4K or 4K Plus model.
  • Power: it is designed to draw power from the TV, though some TVs still need the extra power cable that may be included.
  • The voice remote and which buttons or streaming shortcuts are listed.
  • HDMI and TV compatibility, including whether your set has a free HDMI port.
  • Network requirements. A stable home Wi-Fi connection is required for streaming.

Pros

  • A stick is a small upgrade compared with replacing the whole television.
  • Roku's on-screen setup is familiar to many people and includes a mix of free and paid apps.
  • A voice remote helps with search if you do not want to type on a jumble of apps.
  • HD is enough for many older TVs that cannot show 4K anyway.

Tradeoffs

  • Households with a 4K television and 4K subscriptions will not get that resolution from an HD stick.
  • Adding a stick means another remote and another home screen to learn if the TV already has smart apps.
  • Some televisions do not power a stick reliably from USB, so you may need the plug-in power supply.
  • Streaming quality still depends on your Wi-Fi, not just the stick.

How it compares

The direct alternative on this site is the Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Plus, which is a 4K stick with Wi-Fi 6 and Alexa voice search. Choose the Roku stick if you want Roku's software and HD is enough. Choose the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus if you want 4K, Amazon's interface, and Alexa on the remote. The Apple audio accessories on this shelf, such as the AirPods 5, are for headphones, not for adding apps to a television.

Buying tip

Check whether your TV already supports the apps you use, then choose HD only if 4K is not needed. Also confirm you have a free HDMI port and a plan for power if the TV's USB port is weak.
