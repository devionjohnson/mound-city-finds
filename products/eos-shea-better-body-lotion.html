What it is

eos Shea Better body lotion in Vanilla Cashmere is a 16-ounce whipped body lotion in a sweet vanilla-and-caramel style fragrance. The 16-ounce bottle is a regular-size moisturizer, not a travel mini, and it is meant for everyday use on the body after a shower or whenever skin feels dry. This is a scented formula, so the fragrance is part of the product, not an afterthought.

Who it is for

This lotion suits people who already like the Vanilla Cashmere scent, or who want a lightly sweet, dessert-style fragrance in a daily body moisturizer. It is a weaker choice if you need a fragrance-free cream, if you are shopping only for a face moisturizer, or if you want a small travel tube. People with very easily irritated skin should read the current ingredient list, because added fragrance can be a problem.

Features to check before you buy

  • The 16-ounce size, which is a bathroom-cabinet bottle rather than a purse size.
  • That the Vanilla Cashmere scent is a prominent part of the product.
  • The whipped lotion texture described for this line, which is usually lighter than a thick ointment.
  • Whether the current listing is this exact scent and size, because eos offers many flavors and formats.
  • Ingredient notes if you avoid certain oils, shea, or fragrance components.

Pros

  • A larger bottle is practical if you already know you like the scent and use body lotion often.
  • A whipped lotion is typically easier to spread than a very thick cream.
  • Vanilla Cashmere is a recognizable scent if that is specifically what you are trying to repurchase.
  • It is a body product, so you are not using a small face jar to cover arms and legs.

Tradeoffs

  • It is scented, which rules it out for many fragrance-sensitive households.
  • A dessert-style fragrance is not what everyone wants first thing in the morning.
  • A 16-ounce bottle is bulky for travel.
  • It is not a targeted face moisturizer, and using a fragrant body lotion on the face is not what most people should assume is appropriate.

How it compares

If you want a fragrance-free cream that can be used on the body, the CeraVe Moisturizing Cream in the 19-ounce tub is the unscented alternative on this site. It is a richer cream rather than a whipped, scented lotion. The La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair face moisturizer is a lighter, fragrance-free face cream, not a body lotion. Choose eos if the Vanilla Cashmere scent is the point; choose CeraVe if you want a no-scent household cream.

Buying tip

If you have not smelled Vanilla Cashmere in person, start only if you already know you like sweet vanilla fragrances, and read the ingredient list before you buy a 16-ounce bottle.
