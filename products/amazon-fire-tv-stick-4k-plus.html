What it is

The Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Plus is a 4K streaming stick with Wi-Fi 6 and Alexa voice search, plus access to free and live TV apps on compatible televisions. It is a widely shopped way to add 4K apps to a TV that needs a better smart interface, or that has no smart features at all. This site is not Amazon; the stick is simply one of the products we point shoppers to review on Amazon.com.

Who it is for

This stick suits people with a 4K television who want Fire TV's app catalog and Alexa on the remote, or who want a faster wireless connection than older sticks offered. It is less necessary if your TV's built-in software already does everything you need, and it is more than you need if the television itself is only HD. In that case, an HD stick is the more fitting category.

Features to check before you buy

  • 4K output, and whether your TV and HDMI port actually support it.
  • Wi-Fi 6 support, which can help on a compatible home network.
  • Alexa voice search and the remote layout on the current listing.
  • Power and HDMI requirements, including the power adapter.
  • The Fire TV software experience, which is different from Roku, Google TV, or a TV's built-in apps.

Pros

  • 4K support matches many current televisions and 4K subscriptions.
  • Wi-Fi 6 is a plus on a modern router.
  • Alexa voice search is convenient if you already use Alexa at home.
  • A stick can add apps to an older or app-poor television without a full replacement.

Tradeoffs

  • You will not see a benefit on a TV that cannot display 4K.
  • A second smart platform can feel redundant if your television's own apps already work well.
  • Amazon's software and sign-in are required, which may not be what you want if you prefer another ecosystem.
  • Streaming still depends on a strong home network.

How it compares

The Roku Streaming Stick HD is the other living-room stick on this site. It is HD-only and uses Roku's interface. The Fire TV Stick 4K Plus is the higher-resolution, Alexa-centered option. If your TV is 4K and you want Amazon's home screen, this is the closer match. If you prefer Roku and do not need 4K, the Roku stick is enough. Neither stick replaces headphones such as the AirPods Pro 3; those are for personal audio, not television apps.

Buying tip

Match the stick to the television you actually own. A 4K stick is the right upgrade for a 4K set; an HD television will not gain 4K picture quality from it.
