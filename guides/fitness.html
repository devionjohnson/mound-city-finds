This guide covers the six fitness and everyday-carry items on Mound City Finds. Three are drink vessels: a 24-ounce water bottle, a 32-ounce water bottle, and a 12-ounce coffee tumbler. The others are a set of resistance loop bands, a thick yoga mat, and a pack of disposable hand warmers. They can sit in the same gym bag, but they do not solve the same problem.

How to shop this category

Start with the activity, not the brand. If you want water all day, choose a bottle size you will actually carry. If you want one coffee on a commute, a 12-ounce tumbler is the right shape. If you want to move more at home, decide whether you need a padded floor, extra resistance, or both. Hand warmers are a seasonal add-on for cold time outdoors; they will not help a workout on the living-room floor. As with every item on this site, check the live details and price on Amazon.

Putting it together

A simple starter combo for home workouts is a mat plus the loop bands, with a bottle that matches how much water you like to carry.
