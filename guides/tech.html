This guide compares the six tech items on Mound City Finds. Four are Apple accessories: wired USB-C EarPods, a single second-generation AirTag, AirPods Pro 3, and AirPods 5 with active noise cancellation. The other two are living-room streaming sticks: a Roku Streaming Stick HD and an Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Plus. Audio, item tracking, and television apps are three separate jobs.

How to shop this category

First, pick the category. Lost keys need a tracker, not headphones. A TV that needs apps needs a streaming stick, not AirPods. Then match the product to the device you already own. EarPods and AirPods should fit the port or software you use. An AirTag is built around Apple's finding network. A streaming stick should match the resolution of your television and the software you are willing to live with. Confirm compatibility, box contents, and the live price on Amazon; this site does not sell or service these products.

Putting it together

If you want sound, choose wired EarPods for a simple backup, AirPods 5 for wireless noise cancellation in a standard AirPods shape, or AirPods Pro 3 for an in-ear seal and the flagship feature set. If you want to find a bag or a key ring, look at the single AirTag. If you want to update a television, pick the Roku HD stick when HD and Roku's interface are enough, and the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus when you want 4K and Alexa.
