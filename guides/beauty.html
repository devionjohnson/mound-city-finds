This guide walks through the seven beauty items on Mound City Finds. Two are daily moisturizers, one is a scented body lotion, one is a pack of hydrocolloid acne patches, and the rest are a disposable face-towel pack, makeup remover wipes, and a washable mascara. They can live on the same vanity, but each one is for a different step: moisturize, cover a whitehead, dry the face, remove makeup, or tint lashes.

How to shop this category

Shop by step, not by brand. If your skin is dry, decide whether you need a rich unscented cream, a lighter face moisturizer, or a scented body lotion. If you wear makeup, you need a way to take it off, which is a different product from a dry towel. Patches are only for surface whiteheads. Mascara is a makeup item and will need a remover later. If you have sensitive skin, read the current ingredient list on Amazon, especially on scented or leave-on products. This site does not show prices, so the figure you see there stays current.

Putting it together

A simple unscented pairing is CeraVe for the body and, if you want a lighter face cream, La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair. Choose eos only if you specifically want the Vanilla Cashmere scent.
