Pets buying guide

This guide covers the one pet item currently on Mound City Finds: a 40-pound bag of Dr. Elsey's Ultra unscented clumping cat litter. Because this shelf has only one pet product, the useful comparison is not against another listing here, but against the main types of litter you will see when you shop: scented clay, unscented clay, crystals, and plant-based or paper litters.

How to shop this category

The first question is whether your cats already accept clay clumping litter. A sudden change of material can send some cats to the wrong place, so a restock of what they already use is usually calmer than an experiment. The second question is scent: unscented litter is often easier on cats and people who dislike perfume in the box. The third is weight. A 40-pound bag is a good bulk buy only if you can lift it and store it dry. Scooping still has to happen, no matter how large the bag is.
