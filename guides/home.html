Home buying guide

This guide compares the six home items currently on Mound City Finds. They are everyday household supplies, not furniture and not a full room makeover: bedding, closet hangers, a shower liner, a slim toaster, a cool-mist humidifier, and a hand-soap refill. Each one has its own page with a longer overview. This page is here to help you see how they differ, and which one actually matches the job you have at home.

How to shop this category

Before you open any Amazon listing, name the room and the problem. A tight kitchen counter points to a slim toaster, not a humidifier. A musty shower liner is a bathroom restock. Dry winter air is a different purchase from a sheet set. Also measure once: mattress height for sheets, rod-to-tub length for a liner, counter depth for a toaster, and a clear spot for a humidifier tank you will have to refill and clean. Prices are not listed on this site, so the live figure and options stay on Amazon.
