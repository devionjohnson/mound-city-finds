About Mound City Finds

Mound City Finds is a curated product directory operated by Mound City Capital LLC, a registered limited liability company in the St. Louis, Missouri area. We hand-pick trending products — everyday home, beauty, fitness, tech, and pet items that people are actually shopping for — and list them here with our own short descriptions.

This is not a storefront that takes your payment. We do not sell inventory, ship orders, or process checkout on this site. Each product card sends you to Amazon.com, where you can review the live price, options, and seller details before you decide to buy.

How we choose what appears here

We look for practical items with steady interest, then write original notes about what they are and who they might be for. The list is refreshed as trends shift. We do not show prices on this site, so the figure you see on Amazon stays current.

Mound City Finds is an independent site. We are not Amazon, and we do not claim to represent Amazon. We participate in the Amazon Associates program, and as an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

The company behind the site

Mound City Capital LLC is a registered LLC in the St. Louis, Missouri area. Mound City Finds is one of its projects: a simple, regularly updated shelf of hand-picked trending products.
